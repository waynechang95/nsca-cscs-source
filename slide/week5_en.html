Week 5 Curriculum Breakdown

Core topics and scientific principles covered in this module.

01
Chapters 10 & 11: Nutrition for Health & Performance
  • Macronutrient Guidelines: Protein (1.2-2.0 g/kg), Carbohydrates (3-10 g/kg), Dietary Fats (20-35% energy).
  • Fluid & Electrolyte Balance: Pre-exercise hydration, fluid replacement during events (16-24 oz per lb lost).
  • Pre/Post Competition Fueling: Glycogen loading, post-exercise protein-carb window (3:1 to 4:1 ratio).
02
Chapter 12: Performance-Enhancing Substances
  • Weight Alteration: Safe weight loss (1-2 lbs/week), calorie deficits, protein preservation during cut.
  • Hormonal Ergogenic Aids: Anabolic Steroids, HGH, EPO, Beta-blockers, Insulin.
  • Dietary Supplements: Creatine Monohydrate (loading 20g/day x 5d -> 3-5g/day maintenance), Caffeine (3-6 mg/kg), Beta-Alanine (carnosine buffer), Sodium Bicarbonate.

HIGH-YIELD EXAM PREP

NSCA-CSCS Practice Question

Test your understanding of module key concepts.

What is the recommended daily protein intake for an athlete aiming to maximize muscle hypertrophy?
A) 0.8 g/kg
B) 1.5 - 2.0 g/kg
C) 3.5 - 4.5 g/kg
D) 0.5 g/kg
Correct Answer: B (1.5 - 2.0 g/kg)
Explanation: The NSCA guidelines recommend 1.5 to 2.0 g/kg of body weight daily for strength and hypertrophy athletes.
