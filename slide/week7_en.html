HIGH-YIELD EXAM PREP

NSCA-CSCS Practice Question

Test your understanding of module key concepts.

Which of the following free weight exercises DOES NOT require a spotter?
A) Barbell Bench Press
B) Barbell Back Squat
C) Power Clean
D) Dumbbell Incline Press
Correct Answer: C (Power Clean)
Explanation: Power exercises performed from the floor (e.g., Power Clean, Snatch) should NEVER be spotted due to high velocity and potential injury to the spotter. Athletes learn to drop the bar safely.
